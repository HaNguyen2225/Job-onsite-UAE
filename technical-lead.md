## About the Role

We are looking for an experienced **Technical Lead** to lead our Development and Quality Control (QC) teams, drive technical architecture decisions, and ensure the delivery of stable, scalable, and high-quality technology solutions.

In this role, you will oversee technical direction across Backend, Frontend, and Fullstack development, establish engineering standards, manage technical risks, and align technology decisions with business objectives.

The ideal candidate combines strong backend engineering expertise, system architecture knowledge, hands-on problem-solving capabilities, and proven team leadership experience.

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Technical Direction & Architecture

- Define system architecture, technology stacks, and technical standards for projects.
- Proactively propose technical solutions to improve business performance and operational efficiency.
- Evaluate trade-offs between system performance, development costs, and time-to-market.
- Ensure architectural decisions support scalability, maintainability, security, and business requirements.

### 2. Team Leadership & Quality Control

- Lead and coordinate Backend, Frontend, and Fullstack developers, as well as QC team members.
- Assign tasks, monitor progress, and ensure deliverables meet quality standards.
- Conduct code reviews and establish coding standards, engineering best practices, and technical guidelines.
- Mentor team members and support their technical and professional development.
- Promote collaboration between Development and QC to improve product quality.

### 3. Planning & Risk Management

- Participate in requirement analysis, technical estimation, and sprint planning.
- Identify, assess, and manage technical risks that could affect system stability or project delivery.
- Collaborate with DevOps teams to implement CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, and logging.
- Ensure technical plans align with project timelines and business priorities.

### 4. Problem-Solving & Optimization

- Investigate and resolve complex technical issues and production incidents.
- Optimize system performance, reliability, and security, including authentication and authorization.
- Review and improve operational processes while maintaining effective controls and delivery speed.
- Drive continuous improvements across architecture, development workflows, and system operations.

## Requirements

### Technical Expertise

- Proven experience as a Technical Lead, Tech Lead, or Senior Engineer with strong leadership responsibilities in a startup, agency, or product environment.
- Deep understanding of system architecture, design patterns, microservices, and API design.
- Strong proficiency in at least one backend technology stack: **Golang, Node.js, or PHP**.
- Golang experience is strongly preferred.
- Basic Frontend development knowledge is an advantage.
- Extensive knowledge of SQL/NoSQL databases, caching mechanisms, message queues, and cloud infrastructure.
- Strong understanding of data structures, system logic, and software engineering principles.

### Leadership & Mindset

- Proven ability to lead, mentor, and coordinate technical teams.
- Strong critical-thinking, analytical, and logical problem-solving skills.
- Ability to make evidence-based technical decisions and evaluate architectural trade-offs.
- Proactive mindset with strong ownership, accountability, and product-focused thinking.
- Excellent communication and cross-functional collaboration skills.

### Tools & Methodologies

- Proficiency in Agile/Scrum methodologies.
- Experience with project collaboration tools such as Jira and Discord.
- Familiarity with ERP systems and business operations is an advantage.
- Professional proficiency in English.

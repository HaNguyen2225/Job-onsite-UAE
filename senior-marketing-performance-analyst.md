## About the Role

We are looking for a **Senior Marketing Performance Analyst** to join our Marketing Performance Analysis Team.

In this role, you will collaborate with Digital Performance, Marketing, and cross-functional teams to ensure accurate marketing data tracking, analyze campaign performance, identify optimization opportunities, and provide actionable insights to support business decisions.

The ideal candidate combines strong analytical skills, digital marketing knowledge, and data visualization capabilities, with a proactive, results-oriented mindset.

## Key Responsibilities

- Collaborate with the Digital Performance team to set up and monitor tracking across marketing channels, ensuring data accuracy and consistency.
- Provide reliable data inputs to support analysis of customer behavior, product performance, and esports gaming activities.
- Prepare and deliver daily, monthly, quarterly, and annual performance reports to the Head of Department.
- Evaluate the feasibility and effectiveness of quick-win initiatives proposed by the Analysis team.
- Monitor marketing funnels and campaign performance to identify issues, trends, and optimization opportunities.
- Recommend improvements to campaign strategies based on performance data and business objectives.
- Test and evaluate optimization initiatives against actual results.
- Forecast performance, monitor progress against targets, and provide timely alerts when deviations occur.
- Collaborate with Marketing and other departments to deliver shared objectives.
- Support the Head of Department with additional analytical and operational tasks as assigned.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Data Analytics, or a related discipline.
- At least 5 years of relevant experience in Marketing Performance Analysis, Data Performance Analysis, or a similar role.
- Experience in esports gaming, financial institutions, advertising agencies, or media organizations is preferred.
- Practical knowledge of Power BI, Google Data Studio (Looker Studio), SQL, or similar analytics tools.
- Good command of Vietnamese and English.
- Familiarity with digital marketing and social media platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and websites.
- A proven portfolio demonstrating relevant analytical projects and performance reporting experience.
- Creative and analytical mindset with a proactive, can-do attitude.
- Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively in a diverse and inclusive team environment.

## Benefits

- Competitive salary package based on experience and qualifications.
- Accommodation support, including hotel arrangements and meal packages, subject to company policy.
- Team-building activities and birthday gifts.
- Opportunities to collaborate with cross-functional teams and contribute to data-driven marketing decisions.

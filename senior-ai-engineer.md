## About the Role

We are looking for a **Senior AI Engineer** to design, develop, deploy, and maintain AI-powered solutions for our MarTech platform.

You will play a key role in building backend infrastructure and intelligent services using Python or Node.js, with a focus on Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), and data-driven marketing solutions.

Working closely with frontend developers, data scientists, and product teams, you will help deliver scalable AI capabilities that support business growth and improve platform performance.

## Key Responsibilities

### Data Management
- Collect, preprocess, and curate large datasets for NLP and LLM training and evaluation.
- Implement data augmentation and enhancement techniques to improve model quality and robustness.

### Model Development & Deployment
- Develop, deploy, and maintain NLP and LLM models in production environments.
- Build APIs and backend services that make AI capabilities accessible to internal teams and applications.
- Evaluate model performance and fine-tune models based on feedback, new data, and business requirements.

### Collaboration & Technical Leadership
- Collaborate with data scientists, software engineers, and domain experts to deliver tailored AI solutions.
- Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior team members.
- Contribute to AI architecture decisions and development best practices.

### Performance Monitoring & Continuous Improvement
- Implement monitoring and logging for deployed models.
- Ensure the reliability, scalability, availability, and performance of AI services.
- Stay up to date with advancements in NLP, LLMs, and AI research.
- Continuously improve development workflows, tools, and methodologies.

### Documentation & Reporting
- Document technical methodologies, experiments, model evaluations, and results.
- Communicate project progress and technical findings to stakeholders through reports and presentations.

## Requirements

- Master's or PhD degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or a related field.
- 3–5 years of experience developing and deploying NLP and LLM models.
- Hands-on experience with LLMs and language models such as GPT, BERT, Llama, or Mistral.
- Strong programming skills in Python and familiarity with NLP libraries such as Hugging Face Transformers, spaCy, and NLTK.
- Experience with deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow and PyTorch.
- Strong understanding of machine learning algorithms, neural networks, and statistical modeling.
- Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure.
- Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently and collaboratively.
- Familiarity with backend development and API integration.

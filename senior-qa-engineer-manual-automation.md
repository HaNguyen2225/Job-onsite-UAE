## About the Role

We are looking for highly skilled and detail-oriented **Senior QA Engineers** with expertise in both manual and automation testing to join our MarTech team in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE.

You will play a key role in ensuring product quality by designing and executing comprehensive testing strategies, improving automation frameworks, and collaborating with cross-functional teams. This role also offers opportunities to mentor junior QA engineers and drive continuous improvement across multiple projects.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and implement test plans, test cases, and test scripts for manual and automated testing.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand product requirements and define quality objectives.
- Perform functional, exploratory, usability, regression, and system testing.
- Identify, document, track, and verify bugs through resolution using issue-tracking tools.
- Validate user stories, acceptance criteria, and business requirements.
- Design, build, and maintain robust, reusable, and scalable test automation frameworks.
- Develop, execute, and maintain automated test scripts using tools such as Selenium and Appium.
- Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines to support reliable and repeatable releases.
- Work closely with Developers, Product Managers, and DevOps teams to troubleshoot and resolve issues.
- Mentor junior QA team members and promote a culture of quality and continuous improvement.
- Participate in design and code reviews from a QA perspective.
- Analyze test results and prepare comprehensive reports for stakeholders.
- Monitor quality metrics and recommend improvements to product quality and testing efficiency.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience in Quality Assurance, covering both manual and automation testing.
- Proficiency in test automation and API testing tools such as Selenium, Appium, and Postman.
- Experience with scripting or programming languages such as Python, Java, or JavaScript.
- Strong understanding of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC).
- Experience working with Agile methodologies, particularly Scrum or Kanban.
- Familiarity with Git and CI/CD tools such as Jenkins or GitLab CI/CD.
- Hands-on experience with API testing and performance testing tools such as JMeter or LoadRunner.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, documentation, and communication skills.
- Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams in a fast-paced environment.

## Nice to Have

- Knowledge of cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP.
- Experience with cross-browser testing tools.
- Familiarity with accessibility and security testing techniques.
- Experience mentoring junior QA engineers or leading testing activities across multiple projects.
